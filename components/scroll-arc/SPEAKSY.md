# Scroll Arc — Speaksy use guide

**Lab:** `/lab/scroll-arc`  
**Component:** `ScrollArcScene`

## Speaksy placement

Homepage or product story: white disc rides an arc with beats like **“Calls in Hindi” → “Handoff to humans” → “₹3.99/min” → “Book a demo”**. Scroll becomes the product narrative.

## How

Tune `ARC_BEATS` copy; keep `h-[400vh]` for a controlled pass. Disc position is locked to the same quadratic as the SVG stroke for exact path tracking.
