# Pro Pricing Slider — Speaksy use guide

**Lab:** `/lab/pro-pricing`  
**Component:** `PricingCards` / `CreditSlider`

## Speaksy placement

Map Free / Pro / Business → **Starter / Growth / Scale** with minute packs instead of credits. The stepped line is the hero of `/pricing` — operators scrub volume and watch price update live.

## How

Wire `PRO_STEPS` to minute tiers (e.g. 2k → 20k min). Keep blue fill + white ring thumb. CTA becomes “Get Started (₹X/min pack)”.
